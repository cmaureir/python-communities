This group is for everyone passionate about the Python programming language and its amazing community! Whether you're here to learn, teach, share experiences, or simply enjoy the company of like-minded individuals, Py.Amsterdam is the place for you.

Our Meetup is **organized by the community, for the community**.
Our events are conducted in English.

Py.Amsterdam is part of the [Python Software Foundation's Meetup network](https://www.meetup.com/pro/python-software-foundation-meetups/), and we adhere to their principles of fostering an inclusive and welcoming environment.
Please take a moment to read and follow our [Code of Conduct](https://py.amsterdam/pages/coc.html): https://py.amsterdam/pages/coc.html

Whether you're a Python newbie, an experienced developer, or simply curious about what Python can do, come join us and be part of our thriving community!

We prioritize the local community and building a space for it to get together. That means we will always try to prioritize local speakers and share time together over getting some "star invites." The meetup can be a great place to practice your talks before proposing them to EuroPython or other community conferences.

We are also open to social events or workshops. You just have to want to organize one! :)

Visit our website: https://py.amsterdam/
Follow us on https://fosstodon.org/@pyamsterdam
