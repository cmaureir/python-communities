PyData Leicester is a local community that brings together data practitioners, researchers, developers, and enthusiasts to share knowledge and explore the use of Python and open-source tools in data science, machine learning, and scientific computing. Whether you're a student, academic, industry professional, or a small-to-medium business owner underserved by technology, PyData Leicester offers a welcoming space to learn, collaborate, and connect. We host engaging talks, hands-on workshops, and community events aimed at empowering individuals and organizations to unlock the value of data and open-source innovation.
