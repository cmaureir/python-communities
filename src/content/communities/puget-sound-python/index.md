Welcome! We are a fun and friendly user group dedicated to proliferating a diverse and talented Python community in the Puget Sound region. We are devoted to exploring Python-based programming knowledge, embracing new and experienced members from all walks of life, and helping those members achieve their personal and professional goals.

Our website can be found at [pspython.com](https://www.pspython.com), along with our [code of conduct](https://www.pspython.com/code-of-conduct/).
