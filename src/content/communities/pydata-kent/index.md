PyData Kent is a community-driven meetup that brings together data enthusiasts, professionals, and researchers interested in Data Science, Machine Learning, FinTech, Computer Vision, Data Visualization, Open Source, and Artificial Intelligence. Our events feature expert talks, hands-on workshops, and networking opportunities, fostering collaboration and knowledge-sharing in the data community.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced practitioner, join us to learn, connect, and contribute to the growing field of data science.
