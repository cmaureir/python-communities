PySTL is a community organization dedicated to the Python programming language. Our mission is to provide a platform for people to share their work and/or personal projects that use Python, and to highlight the diverse industries that Python is used in. Do you have a talk or project you want to share? Please email kevinlai31@gmail.com and we can discuss.

**Partners and Sponsors**

* We are an official community partner of the [Python Software Foundation](https://www.python.org/psf-landing/)
* We partner with [St. Louis Code and Coffee](https://www.meetup.com/code-and-coffee-st-louis/) and you can often find us during the biweekly meetups. We also partner with the [STL Go](https://www.meetup.com/StL-Go) meetup
* We are sponsored by [Manning Publishing](https://mng.bz/wZOP), who generously provides us with e-books and group discounts.
