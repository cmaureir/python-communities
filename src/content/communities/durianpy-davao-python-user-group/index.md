**What We're About**
DurianPy is a volunteer-driven, non-profit organization dedicated to supporting and nurturing the growth of the Python programming community in Davao City.

**Our Aim**

* To demonstrate that Python offers something for everyone: hobbyists, educators, students, and professionals alike.
* To inspire individuals in Davao to excel in their craft through Python and open-source technologies.
* To provide a welcoming environment where the Python programming language and related technologies can be explored, discussed, and practiced.

We believe that by fostering environments that inspire and motivate individuals, we can make significant improvements in the tech industry in Davao City, encouraging technical competence and passion for the craft.

**Why Python**
We firmly believe in and support the impact and innovation Python has brought to the technology industry. Python powers the systems, products, and services of companies like Google, YouTube, NASA, Disney, Instagram, Disqus, Pinterest, and Bitbucket.

Python is also a versatile business solution for web development, automation, monitoring, artificial intelligence, data science, cloud computing, and more.

**What we do**
Since our inception, we have organized numerous events and activities to bring together Python enthusiasts in Davao City.

* We host regular meetups, workshops, and public Python training sessions throughout the year.
* We create opportunities for learning, networking, and collaboration among Python programmers of all skill levels.

Whether you're new to programming, an experienced developer, or simply curious about Python, you're welcome to join our community!

**Code of Conduct**
We highly value the participation of each member of the DurianPy community and aim to ensure that everyone has a positive and rewarding experience. Therefore, all members are expected to treat others with respect and courtesy during all DurianPy events, whether officially organized by DurianPy or not.

To clarify our expectations, all participants, including attendees, speakers, exhibitors, organizers, and volunteers at any DurianPy event, must adhere to the following [Code of Conduct](https://policies.python.org/python.org/code-of-conduct/).

**Contact Information**
If you believe someone is violating the Code of Conduct or have any other concerns, please contact a member of the DurianPy Code of Conduct team immediately. They can be reached via email at [durianpy.davao@gmail.com](durianpy.davao@gmail.com).
