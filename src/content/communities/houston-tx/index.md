<p>We are a supportive group of women coders around the Python programming language. We aim to connect with beginners and experts alike as an extension of the global PyLadies community effort &lt;br&gt;</p>
<p>Our goal is to empower women to participate and become leaders in the larger Python community through networking events, education, social gatherings, and outreach efforts.</p>
<p>Join our supportive network of women, and anyone with an interest in Python is welcome!</p>
<p>Email us at: Houston.Pyladies@gmail.com</p>
<p>Please <a href="http://slackin.pyladies.com/">join us in PyLadies Slack</a> in the #houston channel.</p>
<p>We adhere to the <a href="https://www.python.org/psf/codeofconduct/">Python Community Code of Conduct</a>.</p>
