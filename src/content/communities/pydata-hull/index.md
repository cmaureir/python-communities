PyData Hull is an educational program of NumFOCUS, a 501(c)3 non-profit organization. We provide a forum for the local community of users and developers of data analysis tools to share ideas and learn from each other. Our meetups promote discussion of best practices, new approaches, and emerging technologies for data management, processing, analytics, and visualization. We welcome data scientists, researchers, students, and professionals working with Python, R, Julia, and other open-source tools.

Whether you're a seasoned practitioner or just starting your data science journey, PyData Hull offers a supportive environment for learning, networking, and collaboration in the vibrant Hull tech community.

The PyData Code of Conduct governs this meetup ([http://pydata.org/code-of-conduct.html](http://pydata.org/code-of-conduct.html)). To discuss any issues or concerns, please contact the NumFOCUS Code of Conduct Working Group or group organizers.

Please let us know if you need any additional information. We're eager to get started and committed to building a strong PyData community in Hull!
