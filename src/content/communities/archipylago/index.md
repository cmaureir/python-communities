archipylago is your friendly neighbourhood Python community in Turku, Finland. We are a non-profit community that brings together software developers who want to learn more about Python and meet fellow Pythonistas. We organize meetups, sprints, and casual gatherings to bring together the community.

Our community is open for everyone regardless of their background or skill level with programming. We are part of the [TurkuDev](https://turkudev.fi/) collective.

If you want to host a meetup or give a talk, get in touch with us on the TurkuDev Discord.
