We meet monthly to discuss Django, Python, the web, and related topics. The meetups are open to all with an interest in building websites with Django.

We have a website at [djangolondon.com](https://www.djangolondon.com/).

Want to share your Django or Python knowledge? We'd love to feature your talk — fill out our [speaker form](https://ripe-shovel-1a2.notion.site/2a4e5750ee7a8079ae0dda7f2ae83e4a?pvs=149) to propose one.

Our meetups are governed by a [Code of Conduct](https://www.djangolondon.com/code-of-conduct/).
