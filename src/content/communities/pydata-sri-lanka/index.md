Welcome to the PyData Sri Lanka Chapter! We're a community of data enthusiasts, professionals, researchers, lecturers and students passionate about the use and exploration of Python tools in data science. Our mission is to promote the open-source PyData ecosystem, share the latest techniques and methodologies, and foster networking and collaboration among our members. Whether you're an expert looking to share your knowledge, or a newcomer seeking to learn, our group offers a platform for growth, innovation, and community-building. Join us for regular meetups, workshops, and special events that dive deep into the world of data analysis, machine learning, and more using Python. Together, we aim to advance the PyData mission of an accessible and community-driven approach to data science.
