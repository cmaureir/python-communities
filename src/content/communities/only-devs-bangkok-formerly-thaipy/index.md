One of Bangkok's longest-running tech meetups, formerly known as ThaiPy. Join us each month to keep up with the latest programming languages, libraries, and techniques. We have two talks at each event, offer a friendly space to meet like-minded people, and aim for you to leave with something you can use in your own work. Everyone is welcome, beginner to advanced or just curious about tech, whatever your background. 
We recently renamed the group and broadened its focus, because the work of a developer is changing fast. These days we often work across many languages and technologies, and we want to help you keep up.
