PyHawaii is a community dedicated to the growth and use of the Python Programming Language in Honolulu, the Islands of Hawai'i and across the Pacific. Join us for **tutorials**, **projects**, **mentoring**, **sprints** and **activities** throughout the month.

**All skill levels are welcome!** PyHawaii is your source for all things Python: 
• beginner training 
• advanced tradecraft 
• web development 
• data science and visualization 
• automation 
• system administration

At PyHawaii, like PyCon, we are all about community, inclusivity and \`ohana - so come join our meetup, where you will find friends, family and fantastic opportunities to learn, grow, contribute and advance your skills.

If you'd like to sponsor our meetup or donate individually, [please use our donation page](https://psfmember.org/civicrm/contribute/transact/?reset=1&id=23).

Please note that all group members must adhere to [Python Software Foundation's Code of Conduct](https://policies.python.org/python.org/code-of-conduct/).
