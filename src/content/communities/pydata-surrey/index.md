Welcome to PyData Surrey, the local chapter of the global PyData community!

We're a community of AI and Data Science enthusiasts who use open-source tools like Python, R, and Julia to explore data, build models, and solve real-world problems. Join us for talks, workshops, and networking with researchers, professionals, and learners across Surrey. Whether you're new to data or an experienced practitioner, PyData Surrey is here to connect, inspire, and grow together.

We follow the NumFOCUS Code of Conduct to ensure a respectful, inclusive space for all.
