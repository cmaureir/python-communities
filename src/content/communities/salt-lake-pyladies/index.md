<p><b>We are the Salt Lake City chapter of an&nbsp;<span>international mentorship group with a focus on helping more women become active participants and leaders in the Python open-source community. Our mission is to promote, educate and advance a diverse Python community through outreach, education, conferences, events and social gatherings.&nbsp;</span><span>PyLadies also aims to provide a friendly support network for women and a bridge to the larger Python world. Anyone with an interest in Python is encouraged to participate!</span></b></p>
<p>Our meetings have a Code of Conduct:&nbsp;<a href="https://www.pyladies.com/CodeOfConduct/">https://www.pyladies.com/CodeOfConduct/</a></p>
