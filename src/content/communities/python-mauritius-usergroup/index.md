The Python Mauritius User Group (pymug) is the local Python user group of Mauritius. Our aim is to provide opportunities to everybody to upskill their level of Python and to ease industry-adoption of Python-related technologies. A nice overview of the User Group can be found on the MSCC blog [here](https://www.mscc.mu/python-mauritius-usergroup-pymug/). To sum it up, we care about Python education. Our website: [pymug.com](https://www.pymug.com/)

[Our Code of Conduct pledge](https://www.python.org/psf/conduct/)

**About events**

The Pymug organises year-round events, topic days, meetups, from scratch sessions as well as university topic coverage. Have a look at our [2019](https://www.pymug.com/assets/pymug_2019_report.pdf) or [2020](https://www.pymug.com/assets/pymug_end_of_year_2020_v2.pdf) end of year reports to know what to expect from us. Looking forward to meet you ✨.

**Who should join?**

Anybody who wishes to be kept updated of Python activities happening in Mauritius or who just share a love of Python.

**Fees and charges**

The group membership is free!

**Affiliation**

The usergroup uses the [Python Software Foundation](https://www.python.org/psf/) (PSF) 's infrastructures and so is bound to follow the PSF's code of conduct among others.
