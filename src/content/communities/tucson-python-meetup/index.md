<p>Please join us on Slack:&nbsp;<a href="http://bit.ly/tupleslack" target="_blank">http://bit.ly/tupleslack</a></p><p>The goal of this group is to encourage more mingling, learning and sharing ideas within the Tucson Python community. Everybody is welcome, no matter what your level of Python experience.</p><p>We will be organizing presentations and workshops on everything from how to use Python to control the lights in your home, to building web applications which can scale to millions of users.</p><p><strong>Code of Conduct</strong></p><p>The Tucson Python Meetup is dedicated to providing a harassment-free community for everyone. We do not tolerate harassment of community members in any form. Participants violating these rules may be sanctioned or expelled from the community at the discretion of the organizers.</p><p>Harassment includes offensive verbal or written comments. Sexual language and imagery is not appropriate for any event or communication channel.&nbsp;</p><p>Community members asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately.&nbsp;</p><p>Sponsors and presenters are also subject to the anti-harassment policy.</p><p>If a community member engages in harassing behavior, the organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning the offender and expulsion from the community.&nbsp;</p><p>If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, please contact an organizer immediately.</p><p>If you have questions or feedback about this Code of Conduct please contact one of the organizers.</p>
