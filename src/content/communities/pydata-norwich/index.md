PyData Norwich is a community of Python and Data Science professionals in Norwich, UK. The group has origins in the Python User Group, which was part of the NorDev community, and Data Science Norfolk.

We host many events throughout the year, from technical talks and workshops to casual meetups. Our events cover diverse topics in Python, Data Science, and Machine Learning, catering to all skill levels.

You can find out more about us at [norwich.pydata.org](https://norwich.pydata.org), sign-up to our [mailing list](https://norwich.pydata.org/about) and view [previous events ](https://norwich.pydata.org/events)we held as Data Science Norfolk.

The [PyData Code of Conduct](https://pydata.org/code-of-conduct/) governs this meetup. To discuss any issues or concerns relating to the code of conduct or the behavior of anyone at a PyData meetup, please contact NumFOCUS at admin@pydata.org or the group organizer. Find out more about the PyData organisation at [pydata.org](https://pydata.org/).
