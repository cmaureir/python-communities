The Orlando Python User Group is for users, enthusiasts, and students in Orlando, Florida, interested in the Python programming language. We meet twice monthly to discuss topics and projects in Python, practice our skills, and trade stories. All skill levels are welcome.

You can view past talks on [YouTube](https://watch.pyorl.org) and check out our [GitHub org](https://github.com/orlandopython).

Our community is governed by our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/orlandopython/code-of-conduct).
