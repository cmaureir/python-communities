PyWaw gathers Python enthusiasts in Warsaw and the surrounding area, meeting roughly every second or third Monday of the month. The group welcomes students, professional developers, and beginners to exchange experiences and ideas, and has held over 100 meetings with hundreds of speakers and presentations to date.
