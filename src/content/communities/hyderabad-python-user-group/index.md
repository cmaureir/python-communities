**Hyderabad Python User Group (HydPy)** is a community-driven initiative focused on promoting Python in Hyderabad. Over the past nine years, HydPy has hosted regular meetups and workshops, building a vibrant community of more than 20000 members.

We also collaborate with other open-source software (OSS) communities both in Hyderabad and across India to broaden the reach of OSS and Python.

If you’d like to share your expertise by giving a talk at an upcoming HydPy meetup, please submit your proposal here: [http://bit.ly/hydpy-cfp](http://bit.ly/hydpy-cfp).

We invite anyone interested in Python discussions to join our Telegram channel: [https://t.me/HydPy](https://t.me/HydPy).

For any other queries or if you’d like to volunteer, feel free to contact us at contact@hydpy.org or send us a direct message on any of our social media channels listed on our website.

All our events are governed by the [Python Code of Conduct](https://policies.python.org/python.org/code-of-conduct/) (https://policies.python.org/python.org/code-of-conduct/) , and we are committed to maintaining a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone.
