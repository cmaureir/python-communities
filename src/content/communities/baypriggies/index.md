BayPRIGgies is a technical community in the Bay Area geared toward experienced Python developers, hosting talks and tutorials on advanced programming techniques. Distinct from the similarly-named BayPIGgies group, its motto plays on the idea that "the past is sunk, only the future is buildable." It has not held an event in several years.
