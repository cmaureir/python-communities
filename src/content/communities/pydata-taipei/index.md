PyData Taipei 是 PyData 的一個分支，為資料分析工具的使用者和開發者提供一個交流平台，互相分享想法和互相學習。我們專注於資料管理、處理、分析和視覺化等領域的實踐、方法和技術，並主要使用像Python這樣的開源工具。

本聚會的活動均須遵守 PyData 行為準則，我們也致力於在所有活動中促進多元化與包容性。

***

PyData 是NumFOCUS的一個教育計畫，為全球的資料分析工具使用者和開發者社群提供一個讓大家分享想法和互相學習的平台；NumFOCUS 則是美國一家 501(c)3 非營利組織。PyData 社群使用多種程式語言來探索資料科學，包括但不限於Python、Julia 和 R。

***

PyData Taipei is a chapter of the global PyData network, providing a local forum for users and developers of data analysis tools to share ideas and learn from each other in Taiwan. We focus on best practices, new approaches, and emerging technologies in data management, processing, analytics, and visualization, primarily using open-source tools like Python. The PyData Code of Conduct governs this meetup, and we are strongly committed to promoting diversity and inclusion in all our events.

PyData is an educational program of NumFOCUS, a 501(c)3 non-profit organization in the United States. PyData provides a forum for the international community of users and developers of data analysis tools to share ideas and learn from each other. The global PyData network promotes discussion of best practices, new approaches, and emerging technologies for data management, processing, analytics, and visualization. PyData communities approach data science using many languages, including (but not limited to) Python, Julia, and R.
