PyBerlin is an inclusive community for anyone interested in Python - at all levels, from complete
beginners to seasoned professionals.

We'll keep the topics covered as broad as possible - professional development tools, making physical
things, teaching kids to code, cutting edge data analytics, personal projects, and even having fun
and being artistic with code.

We want to be open, inclusive, and very much about community - so please don't hesitate to suggest
meetups, ideas and collaborating with others.

If you want to post about recruiting then please use the jobs discussion board.

If you would like to propose or give a talk, or there is something you want to hear about, please
let us know by adding an entry to this [google form](https://goo.gl/forms/c8yAdEGYRTX7wNyl1).

* Follow us on [Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@pyberlin)
* Follow us on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/PyBerlinPython)

Please take a minute to read our [Code of conduct](https://pyberlin.github.io/coc.html).

Video recordings of our online events and our podcast are available in our
[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZGLKlPfVQJb0qQvbuPaZLw)
