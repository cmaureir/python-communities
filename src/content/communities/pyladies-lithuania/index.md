Welcome to PyLadies Lithuania Meetup!

Are you passionate about Python and eager to connect with like-minded individuals? Look no further! Whether you're a beginner, intermediate, or expert Pythonista, PyLadies is the perfect community for you.

Join us in our vibrant meetup group where you'll have the opportunity to learn, network, and grow alongside fellow Python enthusiasts. Whether you're interested in web development, data science, machine learning, or anything Python-related, our diverse community welcomes everyone with open arms.

At PyLadies, we believe that diversity and inclusivity are key to fostering innovation and creativity. No matter your background, gender, or level of experience, you're warmly invited to be a part of our supportive and empowering community.
