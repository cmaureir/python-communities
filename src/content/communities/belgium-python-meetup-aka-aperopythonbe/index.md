Belgium Python Meetup, also known as Apéro Python BE, brings Python enthusiasts from around the country together on a monthly basis to talk about the language, its modules, and the wider community over a glass of Belgian beer. Its most recent recorded gathering was the Python devroom at FOSDEM, with no events since.
