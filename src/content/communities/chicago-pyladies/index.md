<p>We are a mentorship group for marginalized genders, such as but not limited to non-binary people, trans people, and women, focusing on helping our members become active participants and leaders in the Python open-source community. Our mission is to promote, educate and advance a diverse Python community through outreach, education, conferences, events and social gatherings.<span> <br></span></p>
<p><span>PyLadies of all backgrounds and skills are welcome, from beginner to seasoned Python user, we want to get to know you!&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><b>We welcome everyone, however we expect anyone joining and participating in our group upholds our <a href="https://www.python.org/psf/conduct/">Code of Conduct</a>.&nbsp;</b></p>
<p>Have ideas for events or want to host an event? Tweet us @<a href="https://twitter.com/PyLadiesChicago">PyLadiesChicago</a>&nbsp;or email chicago@pyladies.com.&nbsp;</p>
