<p>Come hang out, learn, teach, and share in the company of other women who program! All who identify as women are welcome. Developers and aspiring developers, please!&nbsp;</p>
<p>PyLadies also aims to provide a friendly support network for women and a bridge to the larger Python world. Anyone with an interest in Python is encouraged to participate!</p>
<p>All Pyladies meetups, events, and forums (including our meetup page, our mailing lists, and other online channels) are governed by the Pyladies Code of Conduct (<a href="http://www.pyladies.com/CodeOfConduct/" class="linkified">http://www.pyladies.com/CodeOfConduct/</a>).</p>
<p>At PyLadies, we welcome all women, inclusive of trans women. Further we would like to extend a special invitation to nonbinary folks, as many (but not all) of the oppressions facing women in our industry(/world) are also experienced by nonbinary individuals. Men are welcome at most meetings if attending as the guest of a female or non-binary identified person.</p>
